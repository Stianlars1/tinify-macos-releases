# Tinify for Mac 1.0.4

A calmer, more compact Activity view - see far more of your history at a glance, and clear the list when you want a fresh start.

## Improvements

- Activity rows are now compact single lines, so a full-height window shows many more items instead of a handful. Each line shows the file, when it ran, and how much was saved.
- Every row action (Reveal in Finder, Retry, Cancel) moved into a per-row menu - click the ⋯ button or right-click the row. Click a finished row to expand its full details.
- A new "Clear activity" button empties the list when you want to. Your Impact totals are always kept, and anything still processing stays put.

## Compatibility

- Requires macOS 14 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.
- Settings, presets, automations, and linked accounts carry over.

## Known issues

- The public Intel build is withheld until separate physical Intel and filesystem validation is complete.
- VoiceOver and full keyboard-navigation coverage for some Optimize controls is still being improved.
