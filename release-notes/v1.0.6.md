# Tinify for Mac 1.0.6

Background processing now comes back reliably right after an app update.

## Fixes

- After an automatic update, the background helper restarts and verifies itself before the app reports it ready - so processing works immediately without a manual toggle. Earlier versions could briefly show "Background processing is unavailable" after updating until the first job was submitted.

## Compatibility

- Requires macOS 14 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.
- Settings, presets, automations, and linked accounts carry over.

## Known issues

- The public Intel build is withheld until separate physical Intel and filesystem validation is complete.
- VoiceOver and full keyboard-navigation coverage for some Optimize controls is still being improved.
